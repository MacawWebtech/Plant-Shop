Home/About
Who We Are

A Plant Shop Built by Plant People

Fernleigh & Co. started on a Saturday market stall with forty propagated cuttings and a handful of seconds-quality pots. There was no business plan beyond keeping every plant we sold alive a year later — and that idea is still the whole plan.

A decade on, we're still choosing every plant by hand, finishing every pot ourselves, and answering plant-care questions long after checkout. We work with eighteen regional growers who share our standards for healthy stock, and every order is packed plastic-free by the same small team that waters the greenhouse each morning.

What We Stand For

Our Values

01

Rooted in Sustainability

Peat-free soil, recycled and plastic-free packaging, and pots that are made — not molded — to last for years, not seasons.

02

Quality Without Compromise

Every plant is inspected for root health and pest-free foliage before it ever leaves our greenhouse.

03

Care Beyond Checkout

Our care team answers plant questions by email for as long as you own the plant — no account or receipt required.

04

Community, Not Just Customers

From beginner workshops to our #fernleighandco gallery, we're building a place for plant people to learn from each other.

Behind Every Order

How We Work

1

Source & Select

We visit our eighteen regional growers ourselves and hand-pick every batch for root health.

2

Inspect & Pot

Every plant is checked for pests, then potted or repotted in our back-room studio.

3

Pack With Care

Custom-fit, plastic-free boxes with moisture wicks keep soil in place and leaves unbruised.

4

Deliver & Support

Your plant arrives ready to thrive, and our care team stays reachable long after checkout.
