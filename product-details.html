Our most-loved statement plant — large violin-shaped leaves that turn any bare corner into a room's focal point. Each plant is inspected for root health and pest-free foliage before it leaves our Portland studio, and ships in a custom-fit box with a moisture wick to keep soil in place in transit.

Water weekly, and provide bright, indirect. Wipe leaves occasionally to keep pores clear, and repot once roots begin circling the base.
