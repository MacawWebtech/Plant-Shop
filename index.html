Our Story

Helping You Create Greener Spaces

Fernleigh & Co. started on a Saturday market stall with forty propagated cuttings and a handful of seconds-quality pots. A decade later, we're still choosing every plant by hand — because a good plant shop isn't measured by how much it sells, but by how many of those plants are still thriving a year later.

We work with eighteen regional growers, finish every pot ourselves, and answer plant-care questions long after checkout.
